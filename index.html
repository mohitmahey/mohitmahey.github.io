Background

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Michigan State University, working with Dr. Timothy Miles on the fungal plant pathogens. I did my PhD in Crop and Soil Sciences at MSU with Dr. Eric Patterson and Dr. Peter Lundquist, where I spent most of my time on a simple question: how does a herbicides work, and what does a plant change to evolve resistance?

That question has taken me from cytology and biochemistry to transcriptomics, molecular docking and population genomics. Lately, from understanding resistance evolution in weeds to the evolution in fungus that attack grapevines and oaks.

I grew up around agriculture in Punjab and studing agriculture before I trained as a molecular weed scientist and bioinformatician, which is probably why I still care what a result means in a field.
